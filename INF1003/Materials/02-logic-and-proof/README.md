# Logic and Proof

Topics 4--7 of INF1003 cover propositional and predicate logic, rules of
inference, and methods of proof. Topics 4--6 appear in Quiz 2 (Week 9, 15%),
while Topic 7 appears in Quiz 3 (Week 12, 15%).

## Topics

| Topic | Title | Key concepts |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Propositional Logic | Propositions, connectives, converse/contrapositive/inverse, precedence, De Morgan's laws, logical equivalence, tautologies/contradictions/contingencies, satisfiability |
| 5 | Predicate Logic | Predicates, universal and existential quantifiers, negating quantified expressions, nested quantifiers |
| 6 | Rules of Inference | Modus ponens/tollens, syllogisms, addition, conjunction, simplification, propositional resolution |
| 7 | Proof Methods | Direct proof, contraposition, trivial/vacuous proof, contradiction, counterexamples, exhaustive proof, proof by cases, existence and uniqueness proofs |
