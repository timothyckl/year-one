## Topics Covered

- Topic 1: Sequences and Summation

- Topic 3: Combinatorics

## Key Revision Areas

### Sequences and Summation
- Identify AP and GP from terms or formulas
- Compute $n$-th term and sum of $n$ terms for AP and GP
- Manipulate summation notation by splitting sums and recognising telescoping sums
- Know the standard sums for $\sum_{k=1}^{n}k$,
  $\sum_{k=1}^{n}k^2$, and finite geometric sums
- Compound interest with annual compounding: $A = P(1+r)^n$

### Number Theory
- Determine divisibility: $a \mid b$ iff $b = ak$
- Division algorithm: find $q$ and $r$ such that $a = dq + r$, $0 \le r < d$
- Modular arithmetic: simplify expressions, test congruences
- Prime factorisation and primality testing (check up to $\sqrt{n}$)
- Euclidean algorithm: compute $\gcd(a, b)$ step by step
- LCM via $ab = \gcd(a,b) \cdot \operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$

### Combinatorics
- Apply product rule (sequential independent steps) and sum rule (mutually exclusive cases)
- Compute $P(n, r)$ and $C(n, r)$
- Recognise when order matters (permutations) vs does not (combinations)
- Subtraction rule: count complement for "at least one" problems
- Pigeonhole principle: $n$ items, $m$ boxes $\Rightarrow$ one box has $\ge \lceil n/m \rceil$
