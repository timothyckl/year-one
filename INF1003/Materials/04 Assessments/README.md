# Assessments

INF1003 Mathematics 1 has five assessment components: ten weekly tutorials,
three quizzes, and a final written examination.

## Assessment Breakdown

| Component | Weight | Coverage | Week |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tutorials x10 | 5% | Weekly, one per topic | 1--11 |
| Quiz 1 | 15% | Topics 1--3 (Mathematical Foundations) | 5 |
| Quiz 2 | 15% | Topics 4--6 (Logic: propositional, predicate, rules of inference) | 9 |
| Quiz 3 | 15% | Topics 7--9 (Proof methods, sets, functions) | 12 |
| Final Examination | 50% | All topics | 14 |

## Quiz Preparation

- [Quiz 1 Revision](Quiz%201/README.md): Topics 1--3 (sequences, number theory, combinatorics)
- [Quiz 2 Revision](Quiz%202/README.md): Topics 4--6 (propositional logic, predicate logic, rules of inference)
- [Quiz 3 Revision](Quiz%203/README.md): Topics 7--9 (proof methods, set theory, functions)

## Final Examination

- [Final Examination Revision](Final%20Examination/README.md): Coverage checklist across all topics and practice strategy.

## Tutorial Submissions

The completed [tutorial notes](../../Notes/README.md) are maintained in
`INF1003/Notes/`. Tutorial solutions are submitted individually to Gradescope
by Sunday 10 pm each week. The supplied trial tutorial can be used to practise
the Gradescope submission workflow.
