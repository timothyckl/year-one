# Mathematical Foundations

Topics 1--3 of INF1003 cover the foundational tools of discrete mathematics:
sequences and summation notation, number theory, and combinatorics. These
topics appear in Quiz 1 (Week 5, 15%).

## Topics

| Topic | Title | Key concepts |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sequences and Summation | Sequences, AP/GP, compound interest, summation and product notation |
| 2 | Number Theory | Division, modular arithmetic, primes, GCD, LCM, Euclidean algorithm |
| 3 | Combinatorics | Product and sum rules, permutations, combinations, subtraction and division rules, pigeonhole principle |
