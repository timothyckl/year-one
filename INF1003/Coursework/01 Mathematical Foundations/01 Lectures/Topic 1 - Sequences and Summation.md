# Topic 1: Sequences and Summation

## Overview

Sequences provide an ordered list of elements generated by a rule. Summation and
product notation compactly represent repeated addition and multiplication.
Arithmetic and geometric progressions are the most common sequence types and
underpin compound interest calculations.

---

## Sequences

A **sequence** is a function from a subset of the integers (usually
$\mathbb{N}$ or $\mathbb{N}^+$) to a set $S$. Notation: $\{a_n\}$ or
$a_1, a_2, a_3, \ldots$

- A sequence can be defined by an **explicit formula**: $a_n = f(n)$
- A sequence can be defined by a **recurrence relation**: $a_n$ expressed in
  terms of previous terms, plus initial conditions.

Common sequences: $n^2$, $2^n$, Fibonacci
($a_n = a_{n-1} + a_{n-2}$, $a_1 = 0$, $a_2 = 1$).

---

## Arithmetic Progression (AP)

A sequence where the difference between consecutive terms is constant.

- General form: $a, a+d, a+2d, a+3d, \ldots$
- n-th term: $a_n = a + (n-1)d$
- Sum of first n terms: $S_n = \frac{n}{2}(2a + (n-1)d) = \frac{n}{2}(a + a_n)$

---

## Geometric Progression (GP)

A sequence where the ratio between consecutive terms is constant.

- General form: $a, ar, ar^2, ar^3, \ldots$
- n-th term: $a_n = ar^{n-1}$
- Sum of first n terms ($r \ne 1$): $S_n = \frac{a(1 - r^n)}{1 - r}$
- Sum to infinity ($|r| < 1$): $S_{\infty} = \frac{a}{1 - r}$

---

## Compound Interest

Initial principal $P$, annual interest rate $r$, compounded annually for $n$
years:

- Amount: $A = P(1 + r)^n$

These are direct applications of geometric progressions.

---

## Summation Notation

Sigma notation: sum from $i=m$ to $n$ of $a_i$, denoted $\sum_{i=m}^{n} a_i$.

**Useful summation formulae:**

| Sum | Formula |
|---|---|
| $\sum_{k=1}^{n} k$ | $\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$ |
| $\sum_{k=1}^{n} k^2$ | $\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}$ |
| $\sum_{k=0}^{n} ar^k$ ($r \ne 1$) | $\frac{a(r^{n+1} - 1)}{r - 1}$ |
| $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} ar^k$ ($|r| < 1$) | $\frac{a}{1 - r}$ |

**Properties:**
- $\sum c a_k = c \sum a_k$ — constant factor
- $\sum (a_k + b_k) = \sum a_k + \sum b_k$ — linearity
- $\sum_{i=m}^{n} a_i = \sum_{i=m}^{p} a_i + \sum_{i=p+1}^{n} a_i$ — splitting

**Telescoping sums:** sums where intermediate terms cancel, e.g.
$\sum_{k=1}^{n} \left(\frac{1}{k} - \frac{1}{k+1}\right) = 1 - \frac{1}{n+1}$.

---

## Product Notation

Pi notation: product from $i=m$ to $n$ of $a_i$, denoted $\prod_{i=m}^{n} a_i$.

**Key formula:** $\prod_{k=1}^{n} k = n!$

---

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing the formula for AP sum with GP sum.
- Using the infinite GP formula when $|r| \ge 1$.
- Miscounting the number of terms in a sequence defined by recurrence.
