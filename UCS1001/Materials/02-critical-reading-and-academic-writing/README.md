# UCS1001 - Critical Reading and Academic Writing

Organised notes for the Critical Reading and Academic Writing block of UCS1001
Critical Thinking and Communicating (Applied Artificial Intelligence). This
block covers the reader response assignment, critical reading, synthesis,
logical fallacies, and academic writing mechanics (paraphrasing, summarising,
flow and cohesion, referencing).

## What this block covers

- The reader response: reading and annotating a discipline-specific text, then
  writing an evaluative response to one takeaway from the text.
- Critical reading as active engagement with a text (purpose, preview, annotate,
  evaluate).
- Synthesising relevant information from multiple sources and integrating
  citations.
- Logical fallacies: recognising and avoiding flaws in reasoning.
- Academic writing mechanics: paraphrasing, summary writing, and paragraph
  flow/cohesion.
- APA 7th edition referencing and evaluating the credibility of sources.

## Assignment context

Assignment 2 is a critical analysis in the form of a reader response: critically
respond to ONE takeaway from a given discipline-specific text. It requires at
least 5 credible secondary sources, cited using the APA 7th edition referencing
convention. Use the Reader Response lecture and Reader Response Essay Structure
guide for writing support. See the
[Assignment 2 assessment guide](../04-assessments/assignment-2-reader-response/README.md)
for the controlling word limit, article-selection process, approval,
formatting, deadline, and submission checklist.
