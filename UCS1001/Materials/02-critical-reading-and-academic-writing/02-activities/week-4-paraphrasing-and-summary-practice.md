# Week 4: Paraphrasing and Summary Practice

Practice tasks to consolidate paraphrasing and summary writing. Work through
them in order. Suggested answers and model examples are given where the task is
demonstrative; otherwise write your own response and compare with a partner or
an AI tool.

## Task 1: Study a worked paraphrase

Read the original text and the model paraphrase, then identify what was
changed.

Original text: "Most people who work closely with animals, such as animal
trainers, take it as a matter of fact that animals have emotions."

Source: Masson, J., & McCarthy, S. (1995). *When elephants weep: The emotional
lives of animals*. Dell Publishing.

Paraphrase: Masson and McCarthy (1995) state that almost all people who have
interacted intimately with animals know that they have the capacity to feel.

Questions:

- Which words were replaced with synonyms?
- How was the sentence structure changed?
- How is the source credited, and which citation type is used?

## Task 2: Paraphrase it yourself

Write a paraphrase of each statement below, then compare your version with a
partner's (or an AI tool's) and provide a reasoned response on which is better.

1. "The current approach to telematics is generally to install a device in a
   customer's car for six months and have it relay the driver's actions back
   to the insurer for evaluation."

   Source: Carroll, P. (2015, September 29). The problem with telematics.
   Insurance Thought Leadership.com.

   Try transforming the sentence from active to passive voice as one possible
   strategy.

2. "By permanently and remotely monitoring the status of key elements of the
   train and infrastructure, maintenance interventions can be planned upon the
   detection of warning information before a fault occurs."

   Source: *The connected journey*. (2016, June 3). *Rail Engineer*.

Checklist for each paraphrase:

- Is the meaning of the original preserved?
- Are the sentence structures and vocabulary sufficiently different?
- Is the source cited (in-text and in a reference list entry)?

## Task 3: Don't change the meaning

The sentence "The woman played the piano with the curved legs" should NOT be
paraphrased as "The woman with curved legs played the piano".

- Explain what goes wrong in the incorrect paraphrase.
- Write a correct paraphrase that keeps the relationship between the woman, the
  piano, and the curved legs.

## Task 4: Classify in-text citations

For each sentence below, identify which of the three APA in-text citation types
it uses (surname + reporting verb; preposition + surname; author and date in
brackets at the end).

1. Moisse (2010) states that children conceived via assisted reproductive
   technology are more likely to be plagued with obesity, hypertension and
   Type 2 diabetes.
2. According to Moisse (2010), children conceived via assisted reproductive
   technology are more likely to be plagued with obesity, hypertension and
   Type 2 diabetes.
3. Children conceived via assisted reproductive technology are more likely to
   be plagued with obesity, hypertension and Type 2 diabetes later in life
   (Moisse, 2010).

Then rewrite sentence 3 using the other two citation types.

## Task 5: Identify reporting verbs in a summary

Read the summary paragraph below and underline all the reporting verbs.

> In the article, "Why the climate fight needs engineers," Shah (2015) has
> stated that engineers are needed to achieve the goal of "zero-waste nation"
> established by the Sustainable Singapore Blueprint (SSB). The author even
> quotes Kuttan, director and country manager of the Clean Technology Centre,
> who highlighted that Singapore has one of the world's "highest carbon
> emissions per capita". The author implies that this is adversely causing
> severe climate change, such as "high temperature and droughts". He also
> expresses a belief that engineers equipped with the fundamental knowledge of
> sustainable energy are needed to counter the current concern. He further
> suggests that in the effort to support this demand, Singapore has initiated a
> scheme to groom all levels of engineers, so as to address challenges caused
> by climate change.

Source: Shah, V. (2015, June 8). Why the climate fight needs engineers.
*Eco-Business*.

Questions:

- List the reporting verbs you found.
- How do the reporting verbs help attribute ideas to the original author?
- Why is the language kept objective (no personal opinions)?

## Task 6: Summarise then synthesise

Write a short summary (one paragraph) of a text you have read recently. Then
rewrite it as a synthesis by combining the ideas with a second source you have
found on the same topic.

Compare the two paragraphs:

- Which one is a summary (key points from one source)?
- Which one is a synthesis (main ideas from different sources, combined to
  support a point)?
- Where does your own perspective or argument appear?
