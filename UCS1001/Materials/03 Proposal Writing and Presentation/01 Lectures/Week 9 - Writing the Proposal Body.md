# Week 9: Writing the Proposal Body

## Overview

The Body is one of the proposal's five required sections. It explains the
proposed improvement, the rationale and objectives, the sustainability balance,
and how the proposal can respond to future change. A separate Methods section
then explains how the improved system will be evaluated.

## Proposed Improvement(s)

In this section you explain your proposed improvement(s):

- What is the proposed modification, solution, or change to be made?
- What is the reasoning for proposing this?
- What specific objectives should the improvement achieve?
- What are the key components?
- Explain the technology to be used in the project.
- Describe the hardware, software, or network components as relevant and as
  understood at this time.
- Include a diagram, graph, or picture to illustrate the problems and the
  proposed solutions, and to show the relationships between them.

## Benefits

Explain what benefits will be gained thanks to the improvement, modification,
or innovation, from the point of view of the key stakeholders.

## Sustainability And Balance

Discuss the implications and contributions of the solution across four
dimensions:

- planet: environmental effects and sustainability;
- people: effects on users, communities, and other stakeholders;
- profit: economic viability and sustainable value;
- principles: ethical responsibilities, fairness, and sound decision-making.

Explain how these dimensions are balanced fairly and compare that balance with
the current system, design, process, procedure, or protocol.

## Adaptability

Briefly explain what changes or adjustments may be needed if the wider context
changes. Relevant disruptions may involve technology, climate, the economy,
politics, or social values.

## Methods

Elaborate on and justify the methods proposed to evaluate the efficiency and
sustainability of the improved system. Consider:

- How might the improvements or changes be fully assessed?
- Aside from benefits, might there be unintended consequences or costs of
  making such changes?
- Taking into consideration all stakeholders and varied viewpoints and
  perspectives, what else can be said about the proposed modifications or
  innovation?

## Team Writing

Work with your group members to write the proposed solution first, then develop
the objectives, benefits, sustainability analysis, adaptability discussion, and
evaluation methods. Check that the Body and Methods remain distinct.
