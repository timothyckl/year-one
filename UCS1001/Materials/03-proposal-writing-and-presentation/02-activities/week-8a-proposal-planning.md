# Week 8A: Proposal Planning

## Overview

Draft the four building blocks of your Introduction section: the background
paragraph, the problem statement paragraph, the purpose statement paragraph,
and the scope paragraph.

## Activity 1: Writing The Introduction Blocks (group task)

Write the following for your proposal:

1. The background paragraph.
2. The problem statement paragraph.
3. The purpose statement paragraph.
4. The scope paragraph.

### Drafting The Background Paragraph

Use the PEEL paragraph framework. The background paragraph should give the
reader the general context needed to understand your topic.

### Drafting The Problem Statement Paragraph

Cover both parts of the problem statement:

- context: what the problem is;
- relevance: why it matters and why it is important.

### Drafting The Purpose Statement Paragraph

The purpose statement should flow directly from the problem statement. It
should be:

- specific and precise, not broad, general, or vague;
- concise, not more than two sentences;
- clear;
- goal-oriented, stating the desired outcome(s).

Consider the typical openings: "The purpose of this proposal is to ...", "In
this proposal, we will describe / explain / review the ...", or "This proposal
will discuss the ...".

### Drafting The Scope Paragraph

State what will be discussed in the proposal (and reasonably what will not).
Include a general description of what will be covered in the body of the
project, an overview of the needs and desired outcomes, and an overview of the
limitations.

Use these questions while drafting your scope:

- What are the boundaries and limitations of this proposal?
- What specific tasks and activities will be undertaken as part of this
  project?
- What is the timeline for achieving the proposal's objectives?
- What resources (for example, personnel, budget, technology) will be required
  to implement this proposal?
- What potential risks could arise, and how can they be managed?
- What are the key deliverables and milestones for this project?

## Activity 2: Critical Dialogue On The Scope (optional extension)

Prompt an AI assistant to provide feedback on your scope of the proposal,
using the questions above. Cross-reference and use evaluative judgment: check
the responses for accuracy, and consider to what extent they align with what
you have learned about scope in this unit. Be ready to share your perspectives
with the class, providing details and examples.
