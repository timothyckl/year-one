# Week 8B: Literature Review Practice

## Overview

Practise identifying the structure and the review strategies used in a
literature review, then plan the organization of your own literature review.

## Activity 1: Identifying Structures (individual work + group discussion)

Examine the example literature reviews provided and identify the structure used
in each:

- **Example 1:** notice how the text is organized. Findings from different
  studies are grouped under subtopics (for example, the time spent writing
  literature reviews, and the amount of revision typically required), with the
  results compared across studies and any challenges to the findings noted.
- **Example 2:** notice how the text traces the development of research over
  time. An earlier finding is presented, then a later study contradicts it,
  then a different approach is taken, and finally the current state of thinking
  is summarized.

Discuss with your group the structures you identified and how they could be
applied to your own literature review.

## Activity 2: Identifying Review Strategies (individual task)

In the provided literature review on wearable technology and physical activity
(organized under the themes of data accuracy, personalisation of data, and
incentives), identify the review strategies used.

Refer to the list of common review strategies:

- contrasting two different interpretations;
- showing the connections between different source texts;
- showing the connections between the literature and the current study;
- pointing out the limitations and potential of different theories;
- pointing out the scope of previous research and the niche of the current
  study;
- pointing out the weaknesses of a particular type of study.

## Activity 3: Planning The Literature Review (group task)

In groups:

1. Identify the organization you will be using for your literature review.
2. Write an outline for your literature review.
3. Use the Paul-Elder framework to help with your proposal writing and to
   improve the quality of your work.
