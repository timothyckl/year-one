# Proposal Writing and Presentation

This block of UCS1001 (Critical Thinking and Communicating) moves from critical
thinking and academic writing into technical proposal writing and its oral
presentation.

In Week 6 the module introduces Assignments 3 and 4: the group research
proposal and the team oral presentation, and it starts preparing the technical
proposal. Week 8 focuses on the Introduction section and the literature review.
Week 9 covers the body of the proposal, the conclusion, and the front and back
matter, including the executive summary. The preparation, design, and delivery
sessions support the team presentation.

These materials cover the AY25/26 Trimester 1 run of the module. Assignment
details, deadlines, and schedules may differ in another run.

## At A Glance

| Item | Information |
|---|---|
| Module | UCS1001 Critical Thinking and Communicating |
| Assignment 3 | 30%; group research proposal; teams of 4-5; not more than 2000 words; due Week 13 |
| Assignment 4 | 20%; team oral presentation of the proposal; delivered in-class in Week 12 |
| Learning formats | Lectures, class activities, MIRO board collaboration, group work, rehearsal |

## Teaching Sequence

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 6 | Assignments 3 and 4; preparing the technical proposal |
| 7 | Recess week (continued group work on MIRO) |
| 8 | Introducing the proposal; writing the literature review |
| 9 | Writing the proposal body; executive summary and front-back matter |
| 10-11 | Preparation, design, and delivery of the team presentation |
| 12 | Team presentation (in-class) |
| 13 | Technical proposal submission |

matching activities with your team. Use the revision summary after finishing the
block, and the reference guides while drafting each section of the proposal or
preparing the presentation.
