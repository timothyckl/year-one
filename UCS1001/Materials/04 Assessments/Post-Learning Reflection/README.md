# Post-Learning Reflection

## Quick summary
The end-of-module reflection of about 500 words, submitted in Week 13
(AY25/26). It is part of the ungraded Assignment 1 reflection component and is
written and submitted in-class in Week 13. Guided questions cover the learning
experience, transfer of learning, growth, and the interactive model of
learning from GenAI tools.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Type | Individual |
| Weighting | Ungraded (part of Assignment 1 - Reflection) |
| Timing | Week 13 (AY25/26), in-class writing and submission |
| Length | about 500 words (plus or minus 5%) |
| Citation | APA 7th edition |

## Purpose
Reflect critically on the learning across the module: analyse how the
experience contributed to learning, how learning transfers to new settings or
other modules, and how you grew; articulate the module takeaways and how you
would use the learning in the future.

## Requirements

- Organise the reflection in unified, coherent, and cohesive paragraphs.
- Move beyond description into critical analysis of the learning experience.
- Cite sources using the APA 7th edition citation style where used.
- The prompts below are suggestions; you are not required to answer all of
  them. Select what is relevant and structure it into logical paragraphs.

### Guided prompts

- Describe the UCS1001 learning experience. How did it enable you to apply or
  transfer your learning to a new setting, circumstance, or another module in
  your degree programme?
- In what ways did the experience increase your curiosity or awareness about
  new content or issues?
- How do you see yourself now compared with who you were at the beginning of
  the learning experience?
- Which aspect of the experience contributed most to your learning, and why?
- What new insights did you develop regarding your career plans?
- How would you describe the engaged learning experience to a potential
  employer in a way that synthesises the knowledge and skills gained?
- Do you feel you will stay connected with this experience, and why?

### Additional distributed prompts

- Discussion-based learning and teaching involved briefly presenting concepts
  and skills, engaging students in discussions, and applying knowledge,
  skills, and attitudes in completing learning and assignment tasks and in
  providing and receiving constructive feedback. How effective was this
  approach in helping you understand and apply the critical thinking and
  communication competencies?
- Engaging in critical dialogue with GenAI tools, asking them to explain,
  elaborate, expand, contextualise, and verify, offers an interactive approach
  to learning. How useful was this model in helping you learn and apply the
  critical thinking, communication, and AAI competencies? How useful were the
  guiding questions for writing project proposals in your dialogue with GenAI
  tools and in writing your proposal?
- How did your interaction with the customised AI chatbot for the critical
  reader response assignment compare with your dialogue with ChatGPT guided
  by the technical proposal questions, in terms of helping you learn and
  apply the required knowledge and skills?
- How do you think you would use this interactive model of learning from GenAI
  tools through dialoguing with them in the future?

## Rubric criteria (plain language)

Same rubric as Assignment 1 - Reflection. Weights: Content and Organization
75%, Language 25%. Levels: Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Minimally adequate,
Fail.

- Content: clear connections to the prompts with relevant content and
  supporting details; a clear focus with depth and breadth; critical analysis
  that articulates the value of the learning to yourself and/or others;
  integrated connections to other courses, past learning, life experience, and
  personal goals.
- Language: awareness of audience and purpose; appropriate style and tone;
  precision, accuracy, and fluency; correct syntax, grammar, vocabulary, and
  spelling; accurate citation and APA 7th edition usage.

## Preparation order

1. During the module, keep notes on what you learned, struggled with, and
   applied, including feedback and module materials.
2. Before Week 13, revisit your module goals and your pre-learning reflection.
3. Review the guided prompts and select those most relevant to your
   experience.
4. Draft a critical reflection in unified paragraphs, connecting experiences
   to growth, transfer, and career insights.
5. Self-check against the rubric and the length (about 500 words).

## Submission checklist

- [ ] About 500 words, organised in unified, coherent paragraphs.
- [ ] Critical analysis connecting the learning to growth, transfer, and
      career insights.
- [ ] Relevant guided prompts addressed (not necessarily all).
- [ ] APA 7th edition citations where sources are used.
- [ ] Standard cover page with the signed declaration.
- [ ] Written and submitted in-class in Week 13 (AY25/26).
