# Assignment 2 - Reader Response

## Quick summary
Individual essay, 30% of the grade, due in Week 6 (AY25/26). A 700-750 word
critical reader response to an approved article on a system, design, process,
procedure, or protocol that needs improvement. The article is selected in a
group of five and must be approved before writing. Requires critical reading,
paraphrasing and summarising, secondary research, a clear stance, and APA 7th
edition citation.

## Purpose
Apply critical reading skills in responding to a text; apply paraphrasing,
summarising, and citation skills; and craft a unified, coherent, cohesive
reader response.

## Requirements

- Identify a specific system, design, process, procedure, or protocol that
  needs improvement to enhance its efficiency and sustainability.
- In a group of five, choose an article of manageable length that addresses a
  relevant issue, from a credible source or author.
- Post the article with the names of all group members on the discussion
  board. Only one group may work on each article; if an article is taken,
  choose another. Wait for the lecturer to approve the article before
  beginning.
- Conduct secondary research on the topic. Based on the type, quantity, and
  quality of existing evidence plus your own reasoning, form a clear stance.
- Write the reader-response essay of 700-750 words.

### Structure (Introduction, Body, Conclusion)

Introduction:

- State the title of the work, the author's name, and the date of publication.
- Briefly outline the author's thesis or claim and the main ideas of the text.
- State your own thesis and your perspective or critical position.

Body:

- Support your stance with specific examples, evidence, and citations from the
  text and credible, recent sources.
- Acknowledge counterarguments fairly, along with their supporting evidence,
  and respond to them thoughtfully.
- In the conclusion or solution, consider and balance multiple core values:
  Ethical (principles and responsibilities), Social (impact on people and
  communities), Environmental (effects on the planet), and Economic
  (viability and sustainability of the idea).
- Present mitigation strategies for the potential short- and long-term
  limitations or negative impacts of your ideas or solution.
- Ensure the conclusion or solution is fair, valid, and practical for the
  context.
- Briefly discuss what changes or adjustments may be necessary in response to
  new developments, disruptions, or changes in the world (for example
  technology, climate, economy, politics, social values).
- Cite these sources in-text and in the References using the APA 7th edition
  citation style.

Conclusion:

- Restate your thesis, synthesise your supporting views, write a concluding
  sentence to drive the message home, and avoid introducing any new ideas.

## Rubric criteria (plain language)

Levels: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Adequate, Failure.

- Connection to the text: accurately and precisely identify key ideas in
  credible, original sources; analyse and evaluate with depth and breadth;
  support a clear thesis and other assertions with significant facts,
  illustrations, or explanations; and draw clear, logical conclusions.
- Source integration: integrate secondary-source information using effective
  conventions, documenting sources correctly in-text and end-of-text in APA
  7th edition.
- Organisation and development: cohesive, coherent, and logical development
  throughout, including the thesis and the response; present and support
  assertions with significant, unified detail; acknowledge reservations and
  respond fairly.
- Language: a significant level of precision, accuracy, and fluency; correct
  syntax, grammar, vocabulary, and spelling.

Strong responses are characterised by accurate paraphrasing, summarising, and
synthesising of source ideas; depth and breadth of analysis and evaluation;
clear thesis support; clear logical conclusions; correct APA 7th
documentation; cohesive development across the summary, evaluation, thesis,
and response; and fair acknowledgement of reservations.

## Preparation order

1. Meet with your group; agree on a system, design, process, procedure, or
   protocol that needs improvement.
2. Shortlist candidate articles; choose one of manageable length from a
   credible source or author.
3. Post the article with all group member names on the discussion board and
   confirm lecturer approval (one group per article).
4. Conduct secondary research; evaluate the type, quantity, and quality of the
   evidence.
5. Form a clear stance and write your thesis.
6. Draft the introduction: source identification, the author's ideas, and your
   thesis.
7. Draft the body: evidence, counterarguments, core values, mitigations, and
   adaptability.
8. Draft the conclusion: restate the thesis, synthesise, and close without new
   ideas.
9. Check APA 7th in-text citations and the reference list.
10. Format and proofread; self-check against the rubric.

## Submission checklist

- [ ] Article approved by the lecturer before writing began.
- [ ] Essay of 700-750 words.
- [ ] Introduction states the title, author, publication date, the author's
      thesis and main ideas, and your own thesis.
- [ ] Body supports your stance with cited evidence; counterarguments
      acknowledged and answered.
- [ ] Core values (ethical, social, environmental, economic) balanced in the
      conclusion or solution.
- [ ] Mitigation strategies for short- and long-term limitations.
- [ ] Adjustments in response to new developments discussed.
- [ ] APA 7th edition in-text citations and reference list.
- [ ] Times New Roman, Arial, or Calibri, size 12, double spaced.
- [ ] Standard cover page with the signed declaration.
- [ ] Softcopy submitted to the LMS dropbox by Week 6.
