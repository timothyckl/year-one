# Assignment 3 - Technical Proposal

## Quick summary
Team project (groups of 4-5), 30% of the grade, due in Week 13 (AY25/26).
Write a technical proposal of not more than 2000 words (from the Introduction
through the Conclusion), addressed to the lecturer who plays the role of a
corporate audience. Includes a literature review, a selected or developed
solution, a methods section, and a sustainability analysis across planet,
people, profit, and principles, in APA 7th edition.

## Purpose
Showcase knowledge, skills, and attitudes in technical proposal writing. Write
in an academic tone and style, cite sources using the APA 7th edition
guidelines, and address the proposal to the lecturer as a corporate audience.

## Requirements

- As a team, identify and elaborate on a specific system, design, process,
  procedure, or protocol that needs improvement.
- Perform secondary research and write a literature review about the system
  and the solutions currently available to improve it.
- Select one or a combination of existing solutions, or develop a solution.
- In the Body of the proposal, explain the solution, the rationales for the
  decision, and the specific objectives to be achieved by the improved
  system. Discuss the implications and contributions of the solution to
  sustainability across planet, people, profit, and principles. Explain how
  these factors are balanced fairly and how that balance compares with the
  current system. Briefly discuss what changes or adjustments may be
  necessary in response to new developments, disruptions, or changes in the
  world (for example technology, climate, economy, politics, social values).
- In the Methods section, elaborate on and justify the method(s) proposed to
  evaluate the efficiency and sustainability of the improved system.
- Write a proposal of not more than 2000 words, counted from the Introduction
  through the Conclusion, with these sections:

1. Introduction
2. Literature Review
3. Body
4. Methods
5. Conclusion

The evaluation criteria also reference supporting elements such as an
executive summary, a guiding research question, background information, a
problem statement, benefits, evaluation, and conclusion. The explicit section
list above is the required core of the proposal.

## Rubric criteria (plain language)

Weights: Content and Organization 75%, Language 25%. Levels: Excellent, Very
Good, Good, Adequate, Failure.

Content and organization:

- Objectives: identify project objectives clearly based on a well-informed
  description and the assignment requirements, and set appropriate priorities.
- Background: identify relevant, valid background information that supports
  the research focus, problem identification, proposed solutions, and
  decision-making, using credible professional sources with depth and
  breadth.
- Design context and analysis: describe the design context fully, identify the
  problem precisely, and analyse the solution. Illustrate design thinking
  through analysis of ideal, gap, and goal. Analyse solution alternatives for
  desirability, technical feasibility, and viability.
- Constraints: identify relevant constraints and limitations, and address and
  analyse them fairly.
- Solutions and conclusions: recommend a logical solution clearly based on a
  valid interpretation of the stated criteria, analysis, and constraints.
  Derive relevant, significant conclusions with logically derived results and
  decisions.
- Organisation: logic and coherence across the report; clearly defined
  sections; effective transitions and discourse markers; key concepts, points
  of view, inferences, assumptions, and implications considered and discussed
  with breadth and clarity.

Language:

- A significant level of precision, accuracy, and fluency.
- Evaluative language that expresses voice and stance (for example
  certainty-uncertainty, positive-negative assessment, expectedness-
  unexpectedness) and organises and links ideas.
- Correct word use, syntax, grammar, and spelling.
- Professional formatting and documentation correctly implemented in-text and
  end-of-text in APA 7th edition; diagrams and visuals enhance and clarify
  the presentation of ideas.

## Preparation order

1. Form a team of 4-5; agree on a system, design, process, procedure, or
   protocol that needs improvement.
2. Conduct secondary research; build a literature review of the system and
   currently available solutions.
3. Select or develop a solution and define the objectives it will achieve.
4. Draft the Introduction: background, guiding research question, problem
   statement, objectives.
5. Draft the Literature Review, synthesising sources in APA 7th edition.
6. Draft the Body: solution, rationales, objectives, sustainability across
   planet, people, profit, and principles, balance versus the current system,
   and adaptability.
7. Draft the Methods: the evaluation approach and its justification.
8. Draft the Conclusion: decisions and significance.
9. Prepare supporting elements (executive summary, references) outside the
   word count.
10. Self-check against the rubric and confirm the word count from the
    Introduction through the Conclusion stays within 2000 words.
