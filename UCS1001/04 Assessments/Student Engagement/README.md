# Student Engagement

## Quick summary
Ongoing individual assessment worth 20% of the grade, spanning the whole
trimester (weeks 1-13, AY25/26). Assesses active participation in-class and
out-of-class, in both oral and written forms, active listening, and
demonstrating respect for others. There is no single deadline; it runs
throughout the term.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Type | Individual, ongoing |
| Weighting | 20% |
| Duration | Throughout the trimester (weeks 1-13) |
| Word limit | Varies with each lesson and task, in-class and out-of-class |

## Purpose

- Learn from others.
- Share ideas and opinions with peers and the instructor.
- Demonstrate your thinking and learning process.
- Improve your critical thinking and communication skills.
- Monitor your own progress.

## Requirements

Engage with classmates and the instructor both orally and in writing
throughout the term.

Oral engagement:

- Be present in class and listen actively.
- Contribute to classroom discussions and initiate dialogue.
- Ask and answer questions.
- Share opinions and feedback; pitch ideas.
- Show respect to others in face-to-face interactions and formal feedback
  sessions, and online via platforms such as Zoom and Microsoft Teams.

Written engagement:

- Complete in-class tasks.
- Provide written feedback to peers.
- Post writing tasks in your learning portfolio throughout the term.
- Respond to course writing assignments.

Possible engagement tasks are provided as the course progresses; specific
tasks may vary by lesson.

## Rubric criteria (plain language)

Levels: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Adequate, Fail.

- Punctuality and meeting assignment deadlines.
- Enthusiastic, active participation in individual, whole-class, and group
  activities; interest and effort to learn and improve.
- Asking questions and contributing comments and ideas.
- Following instructions and responding to others' contributions and
  questions.
- Taking initiative in individual learning, group discussions, and feedback
  seeking and providing; giving quality feedback to others.
- Responding to and enacting the feedback provided by peers and the teacher.
- Completing written tasks.
- Active listening, with close attention to the comments and questions of
  other students and the teacher.
- Showing respect in interactions; regular attendance.

## Preparation order

- Treat engagement as a continuous, term-long commitment rather than a single
  task.
- Attend regularly and punctually; take part actively in all activities.
- Prepare questions or comments before each workshop.
- Complete in-class tasks and written feedback on time.
- Keep your learning portfolio up to date with writing tasks.
- Act on peer and instructor feedback, and reflect on your progress.

## Submission checklist (ongoing)

- [ ] Regular, punctual attendance throughout the term.
- [ ] Active participation in individual, class, and group activities.
- [ ] Questions asked and opinions or feedback shared, in-class and online.
- [ ] Written tasks and written peer feedback completed.
- [ ] Learning portfolio posts maintained throughout the term.
- [ ] Feedback received acted on; respect shown in all interactions.
