# Assignment 4 - Team Presentation

## Quick summary
Team oral presentation based on the proposal, 20% of the grade, with delivery
assessed individually. Delivered in-class in Week 12 (AY25/26). Each member
aims to speak for about 3 minutes; the total time including the Q&A is 20
minutes. The presentation pitches the proposal to the stakeholders it was
written for, rather than simply explaining it.

## Purpose
Pitch the proposal to the same stakeholders for whom it was written, persuade
the audience of the merits of the proposed solution, and emphasise its clear
benefits for their needs. Demonstrate individual delivery skills and effective
team performance.

## Requirements

- Base the presentation content on the proposal, but frame it as a persuasive
  pitch rather than an explanation of the proposal.
- Each member aims to speak for about 3 minutes; the time limit is strictly
  enforced.
- A Q&A session follows each group presentation; each member actively
  participates.
- Individual effectiveness: share content that is significant, relevant, and
  sufficient for the topic; use verbal and non-verbal skills; aim for a
  clear, fluent, confident presentation.
- Team performance: assign equal airtime to each member; show respect and
  consideration for all members; create visual aids or slides that help the
  audience follow; use source material ethically; acknowledge sources using
  the APA 7th edition citation style and include a reference list on the
  final slide.
- Submit the team's slides as a single PDF to the LMS dropbox by the deadline
  on the presentation day. Only one team member submits.

## Rubric criteria (plain language)

Weights: Individual effectiveness 80% (Content 20%, Verbal skills 30%,
Non-verbal skills 30%); Team performance 20% (Team dynamics 10%, Visual aids
10%). Levels: Fail, Adequate, Good, Very good, Excellent.

Individual - content:

- Purposeful, relevant, and engaging content for the topic or section.
- Sufficient supporting details with strong, significant connections.
- Good quality of Q&A responses evidencing sound and in-depth knowledge and
  understanding of the issue.

Individual - verbal skills:

- Competence in spoken grammar and pronunciation; few or no notable lapses.
- Accurate and appropriate word choice throughout.
- Appropriate register maintained.
- Smooth flow with minimal fillers or hesitations.

Individual - non-verbal skills:

- Appropriate pace, loudness, and clarity; effective vocal strategies.
- Eye contact well sustained with all parts of the audience.
- Appropriate facial expressions and gestures; confident, enthusiastic body
  language.
- Confident, professional use of physical space and handling of visual aids.

Team - dynamics:

- High level of professionalism in interaction and handover processes.
- Strong connections across sections with effective use of cohesive devices.
- Engagement from all members; positive team appearance including facial
  expression and body language.

Team - visual aids:

- Appealing, well-designed slides that are easy to read and understand.
- Appropriate choice of colours, fonts, and layout.
- All sources appropriately acknowledged; strict adherence to the required
  citation convention (APA 7th edition).

## Preparation order

1. Agree on the content split and equal airtime; map each section to a member.
2. Build slides that follow the proposal but frame it as a persuasive pitch.
3. Rehearse timing: about 3 minutes per member, with smooth handovers.
4. Prepare for the Q&A as a team; practise answering questions on the solution.
5. Check citation conventions and ensure the reference list appears on the
   final slide.
6. Dry-run the delivery and refine pacing, eye contact, gestures, and visuals.
7. Submit the slides as a single PDF (one team member) on the presentation
   day.
