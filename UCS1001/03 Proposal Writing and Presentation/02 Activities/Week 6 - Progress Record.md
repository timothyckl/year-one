# Week 6: Progress Record

## Purpose

Use this document to record your team's discussions and findings as you develop
your technical proposal. The objective is to help your team track its progress.

Update the record after each group discussion. It feeds directly into the
analysis tasks on the MIRO board and into the writing of the proposal sections
in later weeks.

## Preliminary Investigation

### Identifying The Problem (And The Factors Contributing To The Problem)

Record the problem your team has chosen to focus on, and note the factors that
contribute to it:

- What is the problem, in general terms?
- What is the existing process like?
- What gaps have been identified in the existing process?
- What are the root causes of these gaps?

### Research Plan (Both Secondary And Primary Research)

Record your plan for gathering information:

- Secondary research: what sources will you search, and how?
- Primary research: what data or observations will you collect, and how?

### Possible Solutions (And Reasons For Their Viability)

List the possible solutions your team has considered and the reasons for their
viability:

- What solutions are currently available in the market?
- What is your team proposing to improve the process?
- What is the novelty or appeal of the approach recommended?

### Outstanding Issues That Need Resolution Or Further Investigation

Note any open questions that still need to be resolved, such as missing
information, uncertain feasibility, or issues that require further
investigation.
