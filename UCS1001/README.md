# UCS1001 Critical Thinking and Communicating

UCS1001 develops critical thinking through reflective writing, critical reading,
academic writing, technical proposal development, and oral presentation. The
module moves from analysing thought and communication to evaluating evidence,
synthesising sources, proposing improvements, and presenting recommendations to
a specified audience.

These materials cover the AY25/26 Trimester 1 run. Assessment requirements and
schedules may differ in another run.

## Learning Focus

By working through the module material, students develop the ability to:

- apply the Paul-Elder framework to analyse reasoning and communication;
- recognise interconnections, wider contexts, and feedback loops;
- reflect critically on learning, assumptions, and personal development;
- read actively and evaluate claims, evidence, reasoning, and source quality;
- paraphrase, summarise, synthesise, and cite information ethically;
- write unified, coherent, and cohesive academic paragraphs;
- identify logical fallacies and avoid weak reasoning;
- develop a research-informed technical proposal for a specified audience;
- evaluate alternatives, constraints, sustainability, and implementation; and
- design and deliver a clear, persuasive team presentation.

## Suggested Workflow

1. Read each week's lecture notes before attempting its activities.
2. Use the revision notes to consolidate a completed block.
3. Use reference guides while writing, evaluating evidence, or preparing a
   presentation.
4. Open the relevant assessment guide before planning a submission.
5. Check the assignment against its rubric and checklist before submission.

## Academic Integrity And AI

- Cite and reference borrowed ideas accurately using APA 7.
- Distinguish quotation, paraphrase, summary, and synthesis.
- Check the credibility and accuracy of information before relying on it.
- Treat AI output as material that requires critical evaluation, verification,
  and responsible use under current institutional and assignment rules.
- Do not submit work that you cannot explain or defend.
